# Lines

There's a bunch of stuff in this book about the fingers and areas of the hand as they relate to the planets, finger shape etc, that I feel like will muddy the simplicity of the hand lines-sliders idea.

So far, the lines of the palm ennumerated-

- The Line of the Heart
- The Line of the Head
- The Line of Life

Accessory lines-

- Line of Saturn (oh there is definitely some racist stuff in the text here, I was wondering how long they would take)
- Line of the Sun
- Hepatic Line (Line of the Liver)

But a broken line is imporant as is a long/short one. How do I work with that?

I think I should definitely label the hand areas with planets etc "mount of venus" etc. I can probably get this whole cloth from a diagram rather than pick it out of a text, but it's nice to read all the complex symbolic reasoning for flavor, even if the story output is totally random, players may assume it is working from a reasoned system.
There is stuff in this reasoning that should definitely be in the output, regardless of the slider posistions... "injury to the head," lots of specific fortunes...

I think maybe that if there are separate sliders for the lines there could be another control (dropdowns? another slider) for things like breaks or branchiness, redness, but does that too-far interfere with the direct correlation of slider position and length of a hand line? does it encourage too much the player to look up a palmistry book online and try to tailor a hand and see if it matches? Also this should be kept super simple. 4 weeks to show something off.
