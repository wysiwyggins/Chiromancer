# More Text

I've spent the last couple of days experimenting by writing our fate specimens using as much corpora as I could and added or changing corpora when needed. A few discoveries.

The initial tense idea (you were born, you will die) didn't really work. It didn't harmonize well with GAME OVER, and I found quickly that just writing the whole thing past tese felt better. Also, happy endings don't work well. Fortune is different than fate here, happiness and auspicious beginnings are better as a temptation to hubris capped off by a dumb death or a fall from grace. I sketched out a few ideas about how the lines may relate to templates, but I haven't landed on anything solid yet. The line of the head and line of the heart could be used together for the total number of templates? Line of Life, Mars and Fortune could decide on subtemplates and the rest of the lines could be decorative. 

I added a few text specimens to assets/grammar ahead of figuring our the grammar.
